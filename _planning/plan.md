# PLAN

Last updated: 2026-05-25

## Objective

Establish a repeatable research workflow for datasets in `data/`, with clear progress tracking and final reports in `results/`.

## Phases

1. Intake
- Review `data/datasets_info.md`.
- Confirm research questions and success criteria.

2. Profiling
- Generate dataset summaries (shape, schema, completeness).
- Capture baseline observations in `output/`.

3. Analysis
- Run comparative and exploratory analysis from multiple angles.
- Record progress in `output/status.md` with timestamps.

4. Reporting
- Produce final outputs in `results/` as `.md` and `.html`.
- Document assumptions, caveats, and reproducibility notes.

## Open Questions

- Which specific research questions should be prioritized first?
- Are there mandatory metrics, thresholds, or visualizations?
- What is the expected final audience (technical vs non-technical)?
